#### Tournaments
A tournament is a directed grpah, $T = (V, E)$, so that for every distinct pair of vertices $u \ne v$, either $(u, v) \in E$ or $(v, u) \in E$, but not both.

$(u, v) \in E$ can be written as $u \to v$.
