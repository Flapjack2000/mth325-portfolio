#### Conjecture
* Conjecture 1 from [[Proofs/Dr. Wells' List of Conjectures\|Dr. Wells' List of Conjectures]].
In a graph $G$, if there are at least $2$ distinct paths from a vertex $u$ to another vertex $v$, then $G$ contains a cycle.
