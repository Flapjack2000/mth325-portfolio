#### Conjecture
>Conjecture 17 from [[Proofs/Dr. Wells' List of Conjectures\|Dr. Wells' List of Conjectures]]

>For every positive integer $n$, if $T$ is a tournament with $n$ vertices then there is at least one admissible ranking. That is, there is a sequence of vertices $v_1,v_2,…,v_{n−1},v_n$ so $v_i \to v_{i+1}$ for $i=1,2,…,n−1$.

#### Initial Thoughts
* Tournaments are directed graphs
* One directed edge between each pair of vertices
